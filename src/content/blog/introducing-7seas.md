Scientific progress increasingly depends on computation. We use models to interpret observations, explore complex systems, and ask questions that would otherwise remain out of reach. Modern AI expands those possibilities, while raising questions about resources, reproducibility, and access.

7SEAS Lab brings together researchers working on scalable and efficient AI for science. Our shared ambition is to accelerate research, improve efficiency, and enable scalable scientific innovation.

## A research compass

Our concept connects seven algorithmic principles: probabilistic, bio-inspired, distributed, sparse, differentiable, interpretable, and open. Together, they describe the kinds of methods we want to develop and the values that guide how we share them.

Those principles meet seven fields of application: energy, Earth, space, matter, materials, life sciences, and medicine. The scientific questions differ across these fields, but many computational challenges are shared.
